Programme privacy / version 2026-10-02-v1

Your learning. Your choices.

Foundations of Electric Vehicle Engineering is an independent, non-credit-bearing Short Learning Programme provided by Prof Bonginkosi Thango through Thangotech Engineering. This notice describes the data collected by the programme application, not by other services you may visit.

The registration service has not yet been confirmed as open. You can read the programme notes without registering.

Who is responsible?

Thangotech Engineering is the programme provider and the responsible party for the programme records it collects. Contact: to be supplied before registration opens. The provider must complete its operator, retention, security and information-officer arrangements before inviting registrations.

What is necessary for a course record?

To keep an assessed record, the service uses your email account, an internal account identifier, confirmation that you are at least 18, your acknowledgement of the programme scope, assessment responses and results, and submission dates. Your chosen name is requested only to issue a certificate. An email sign-in confirms control of an inbox; it does not independently confirm your legal identity.

The account and assessment data are used to administer the programme, restore your progress, record your outcomes, deliver sign-in codes and issue or verify completion records. They are not used for marketing by this application. The provider must document the applicable lawful basis for these necessary processing activities; optional demographic consent is separate.

Optional demographic information

You may choose to provide country of residence, South African province, age band, learner category, preferred learning language and how you found the programme. These answers help the provider understand educational reach and improve access. You can skip every question or choose “Prefer not to say”. Your decision does not affect access, marks or eligibility for a certificate.

Race/ethnicity collection starts disabled. If the provider enables it after documenting a specific lawful purpose, the question displays that purpose and requests a separate voluntary consent. Race is self-reported only. The programme does not infer race, age or location from a name, photograph, address, IP address or other behaviour.

The application does not request an ID number, exact date of birth, home address, GPS position, health history, financial information or biometric identification. Preferred learning language is a planning question; the current notes are in English.

Who can see the information?

The authenticated learner can see their own programme record. The programme owner’s dashboard shows aggregate counts and statistics, not names, email addresses or individual demographic rows. The backend restricts that dashboard to the configured owner account after authenticator verification. Authorised infrastructure administrators and contracted service operators may have access needed to operate, secure and support the system. Records are not placed in the public code repository.

Combining small demographic groups can identify people. Dashboard categories with 1–9 responses are withheld and a further category is suppressed where needed. The dashboard has no cross-demographic filters. These measures reduce risk; they do not make the underlying account-linked database anonymous.

Certificates and sharing

The certificate contains your supplied name, programme title, issue date, record ID and the provider’s signature. Downloading and sharing it is your choice. Someone checking a certificate must supply its random token or record ID and the printed name. A successful check returns status and programme details, not stored names, marks or demographic information. Deleting your course record stops verification.

Storage and service operators

The public reading site can be hosted on GitHub Pages. Authentication, database processing and certificate generation use the provider’s configured Supabase project; sign-in email may use an additional email operator. Your hosting and email service may log IP addresses and request metadata for security and operations even though this programme does not use them to infer demographics. The provider must document the selected hosting region, operators and any international-transfer safeguards before launch.

Retention, access and withdrawal

Programme policy for this release: remove optional demographic answers after 12 months from their last update unless you renew consent; remove detailed attempt responses after 12 months while retaining result summaries; keep enrolled progress and certificate records for up to 5 years from enrolment unless a shorter period is requested or a specific lawful requirement applies. Administrative and consent audit events are reviewed annually. The provider must run and monitor the maintenance schedule; merely publishing this notice does not execute deletion. Backups follow the operator’s separate documented retention schedule.

Within My record you can review your demographic answers, export your own course record, withdraw demographic consent, or delete your course record. Withdrawal removes the demographic values while preserving your learning pathway. Deleting the course record removes course progress, assessments, demographics and the certificate entry. The separate email sign-in account remains until the provider removes it; contact the provider for complete account removal, corrections, access requests or a processing objection.

The provider should respond to requests and arrange deletion from appropriate operational systems, subject to applicable legal and backup limitations. No anonymous proof of national reach is promised by this system.

Children and use of the notes

This assessed pilot accepts registrations only from people who confirm they are 18 or older. Anyone can read the public learning notes without registering. A supervised school version with the required competent-person consent and child-protection arrangements is not included in this release.

Questions or complaints

Contact the programme provider first for assistance. You may also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa about personal-information concerns. Official information is available from the Information Regulator.

No registration or demographic data is sent simply by reading this notice. Browser requests to the site’s hosting service still occur. This programme is not an accredited qualification or authority to work on high-voltage equipment.
